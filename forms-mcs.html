MCS Pension Forms

Criteria for Submission of Pension Papers by the Retired MCS Officers

  • 3(three) copies of joint photographs, with wife (to be pasted in a plain paper)
  • 3 (three) copies of single photograph for commutation of pension. (- Do -)
  • No Demand Certificate from the EO, Shillong.
  • No Demand Certificate from this Department
  • Copy of latest GPF Statement of Account No.
  • Slip showing particulars of height, identification marks duly attested. 3 (THREE) COPIES
  • Specimen signature 3 (THREE) COPIES

Criteria for Submission of Final Payment of Balances in the GPF by the retired MCS officers

  • 1. FORM-A
  • 2. Latest GPF statement
  • 3. Form of Nomination
  • 4. Attested specimen signatures ( 3 COPIES)
  • 5. Particular of Height and Identification Marks.
