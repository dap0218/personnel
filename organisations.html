Our Organisations

The Personnel & Administrative Reforms Department is the Administrative Department of the commissions and institutions below. It also services all District and Sub-Divisional Establishments and the Governor's Secretariat.

Commissions

  • MPSC

    Meghalaya Public Service Commission

    Recruitment to State services and posts of the Government of Meghalaya.

    Visit the MPSC website (opens in a new window)
  • MSIC

    Meghalaya State Information Commission

    Hears appeals and complaints under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

    Visit the MSIC website (opens in a new window)
  • MSPSDC

    Meghalaya State Public Services Delivery Commission

    Oversees the time-bound delivery of notified public services to citizens.

    Visit the MSPSDC website (opens in a new window)

Institutions and Establishments

  • MATI

    Meghalaya Administrative Training Institute

    Training of officers and staff of the Government of Meghalaya.

  • Raj Bhavan

    Governor's Secretariat

    Officers and staff of the Governor's Secretariat are serviced by the Department.
